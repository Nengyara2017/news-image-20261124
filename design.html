TUESDAY, 24 NOVEMBER 2026
Strait of Hormuz · War at Sea

Iran Says Mines Hit Tankers in the Strait of Hormuz

Iran’s Revolutionary Guard navy says three to four oil tankers hit mines and caught fire in the Strait of Hormuz. Britain’s UKMTO confirmed three vessels were struck by unidentified projectiles on Monday; crews are safe and the ships are sailing on. Iran vows to use “all available means” against the US naval blockade of its ports, as the US Treasury sanctioned 20-plus Iranian exchange houses and the rial slid to about 2.82 million per dollar.

Gaza · Middle East

Israeli Strikes Kill Hamas Security Chief

Israeli airstrikes on Tuesday killed Hamas internal security chief Naeem Abu Nahel and six others, including two police officers, medics and police say.

Ukraine · Zaporizhzhia

Zaporizhzhia Strike Kills 2, Wounds 40

A Russian strike overnight hit a five-storey block, killing two and wounding 40, including four children — one an infant — governor Mykhailo Semikin says.

Nepal · Disaster

5.6 Quake Shakes Nepal Near Tibet Border

A magnitude 5.6 quake struck near Olangchung Gola, Taplejung, at 7:17am Tuesday. Felt in Kathmandu, India, Bhutan and Bangladesh; no damage or casualties reported.

United States · Markets

Nvidia’s Huang Apologises a Day After CEO Return

Jensen Huang apologised to staff in an hour-long meeting on Tuesday, a day after Nvidia reinstated him as CEO. Shares jumped 31% on Monday, adding about $950bn — the biggest one-day gain ever.

Myanmar · Justice

14 Nations Back ICJ Ruling on Rohingya Genocide

Fourteen nations backed the International Court of Justice ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya. The UK formally recognised the genocide and sanctioned five generals.
